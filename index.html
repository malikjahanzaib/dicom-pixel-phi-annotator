What is exported?

Native-pixel boxes grouped by combo and image size. Boxes from all samples and frames are merged; exact duplicates are removed. Each annotated file needs a combo ID. Use Workspace → Save annotation backup to keep frame ownership.

Turn on Redaction preview to fill that merged layout opaque over the open image and confirm it covers the burned-in text.
